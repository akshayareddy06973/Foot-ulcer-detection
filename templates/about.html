About Foot Ulcer Detection

Overview

Foot Ulcer Detection is an AI-driven platform designed to help diabetic patients monitor and detect early signs of foot ulcers. Diabetes can reduce blood circulation and nerve sensitivity in the feet, making injuries or infections harder to detect early. Left untreated, these ulcers can become severe, sometimes leading to infections or amputations.

Our system leverages advanced image processing and artificial intelligence algorithms to analyze foot images uploaded by users. The AI model identifies critical features such as skin texture, discoloration, small cuts, swelling, or wounds, and gives early alerts to the user. By enabling patients and caregivers to act promptly, the platform plays a vital role in preventing serious complications.

Purpose

The primary goal is to empower diabetic patients with a convenient and accurate tool for early foot ulcer detection. Foot ulcers are a major cause of hospitalization in diabetic patients, and early identification can significantly reduce healthcare risks and costs.

With this platform, users can monitor their feet regularly, detect issues at an early stage, and take necessary preventive actions. Additionally, it helps healthcare providers by providing a digital record of foot images and analysis results, which can assist in diagnosis and treatment planning.

Benefits

Early Detection: detection of foot ulcers at the earliest stage, reducing risks.
Preventive Care: Encourages regular monitoring and proactive health management.
Convenience: Users can perform checks from home without frequent hospital visits.
Education and Awareness: Increases understanding of diabetic foot complications.
Support for Caregivers: Members and healthcare providers can monitor and assist patients effectively.
Healthcare Costs: Early detection prevents hospitalizations and costly treatments.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower patients with AI tools that simplify healthcare monitoring, improve early diagnosis, and prevent complications. We aim to create a user-friendly platform accessible to everyone, fostering proactive health management and patient education.

Technology & AI

Our platform uses state-of-the-art deep learning algorithms trained on thousands of foot images to detect early signs of ulcers. The AI continuously improves as more data is collected, ensuring higher accuracy over time. The system identifies subtle changes in skin texture, color, and wounds that are often missed by the human eye.

Future Goals

We plan to expand the platform to include integration with wearable devices for continuous foot monitoring, multilingual support, and educational resources for patients and caregivers. Our goal is to make foot health monitoring accessible and easy for everyone.
